# English Technical Semester Assessment — Draft v9 (Questions 161–180)

Статус: черновик для предметной и методической экспертизы. Не импортировать в защищённый банк и не показывать студентам до проверки. Задания проверяют английский язык, чтение технических сообщений и профессиональную коммуникацию; они не заменяют утверждённые производственные инструкции.

## Questions

**161.** Choose the correct completion: “The quality team ___ the samples before the report was issued.”
- A. have examine
- B. examining
- C. had examined
- D. has examining

**162.** What does *batch number* identify?
- A. A particular production batch or lot
- B. The employee's monthly salary
- C. The dimensions of a technical drawing
- D. The time of a daily meeting

**163.** Complete the sentence: “Please notify the project manager ___ any unexpected change.”
- A. of
- B. to
- C. at
- D. into

**164.** A maintenance note says: “The fault could not be reproduced during the follow-up test.” What does this mean?
- A. The fault was deliberately made worse
- B. The same fault did not occur again under the follow-up test conditions
- C. The follow-up test was not performed
- D. The equipment was permanently repaired in every condition

**165.** Which phrase is most appropriate when asking a colleague to clarify an ambiguous requirement?
- A. “Your requirement makes no sense, fix it.”
- B. “I clarify you the load yesterday.”
- C. “What means nominal load it?”
- D. “Could you clarify what is meant by ‘nominal load’ in this section?”

**166.** What is *version control* used for in engineering or software projects?
- A. Measuring the physical weight of a component
- B. Setting the temperature of a workshop
- C. Tracking changes to files and identifying different revisions
- D. Replacing the need for technical review

**167.** Choose the correct completion: “A list of approved suppliers ___ available in the quality system.”
- A. are
- B. have
- C. is
- D. be

**168.** A technical specification says that a connector has 8 pins. The inspection record reports 8 pins. Which conclusion is supported?
- A. The connector is guaranteed to meet every other requirement
- B. The connector has twice the specified number of pins
- C. The recorded pin count matches the specified pin count
- D. The inspection record proves the connector's service life

**169.** What does *contingency plan* mean?
- A. A plan prepared for a possible unexpected event or disruption
- B. A list of routine product dimensions
- C. A record of completed staff training only
- D. A drawing showing the normal product layout

**170.** Complete the sentence: “The supplier has not yet confirmed ___ the revised parts will be available.”
- A. when
- B. which
- C. whose
- D. whom

**171.** Which sentence is the clearest description of a trend?
- A. “Temperature is a thing and the graph is there.”
- B. “The temperature has many colours.”
- C. “The observation period was measured by temperature.”
- D. “The measured temperature increased gradually over the observation period.”

**172.** What is the purpose of a *technical query* (TQ) in a project?
- A. To order refreshments for a project meeting
- B. To replace all approved design documents
- C. To record annual leave
- D. To formally request clarification or resolution of a technical issue

**173.** Choose the correct completion: “The equipment must remain unavailable ___ the inspection has been completed and the status formally recorded.”
- A. during
- B. despite
- C. until
- D. whereas

**174.** A document states: “The measured value is within the acceptance range.” What does this indicate?
- A. The value is necessarily equal to the nominal value
- B. The measurement was not recorded
- C. The acceptance limits have been removed
- D. The value falls between the defined acceptance limits

**175.** What does *cross-functional team* mean?
- A. A team that works only with one type of document
- B. A team whose members represent different functions or areas of expertise
- C. A team with no assigned responsibilities
- D. A team that meets only once a year

**176.** Choose the correct reported-speech form: Direct statement: “The test starts at 9:00.” Reported later in the past: “The coordinator said that the test ___ at 9:00.”
- A. start
- B. started
- C. starting
- D. have start

**177.** What does *data integrity* refer to?
- A. The visual design of a spreadsheet
- B. The accuracy, consistency and reliability of data throughout its lifecycle
- C. The number of people who can read a report
- D. The speed at which a printer produces pages

**178.** A procurement note says: “Equivalent alternatives may be considered subject to engineering approval.” What condition applies?
- A. An alternative requires engineering approval before it is accepted
- B. Any alternative is automatically accepted
- C. Only the original supplier may be contacted
- D. Engineering approval is prohibited

**179.** Complete the sentence: “The two reports differ ___ the date and the recorded equipment revision.”
- A. at
- B. in
- C. for
- D. over

**180.** Which summary is best supported by the following note? “The software update resolved the display issue. The team will continue monitoring the system for one week.”
- A. The entire system has been proven free of all defects
- B. The update was cancelled
- C. Monitoring ended before the update was installed
- D. The display issue was resolved by the update, and monitoring will continue for a week

## Teacher key and rationale

| No. | Key | Rationale |
|---:|:---:|---|
| 161 | C | Past perfect marks the examination as completed before the report was issued. |
| 162 | A | A batch or lot number identifies a defined production group. |
| 163 | A | The collocation is “notify someone of” a change. |
| 164 | B | The fault was not observed again under the stated follow-up conditions; this does not prove it can never recur. |
| 165 | D | It is a polite, precise request for clarification. |
| 166 | C | Version control records changes and helps identify revisions. |
| 167 | C | The head noun “list” is singular, so the verb is “is”. |
| 168 | C | The evidence supports only the pin-count comparison, not overall conformity or service life. |
| 169 | A | A contingency plan addresses a possible disruption or unexpected event. |
| 170 | A | “When” introduces the indirect question about timing. |
| 171 | D | The sentence clearly describes a gradual change over time. |
| 172 | D | A technical query is used to seek formal clarification or resolution of a technical matter. |
| 173 | C | “Until” expresses the condition that must be met before the status changes. |
| 174 | D | An acceptance range is the interval between defined acceptance limits. |
| 175 | B | Cross-functional teams combine people from different organizational or technical functions. |
| 176 | B | Backshift in reported speech changes “starts” to “started” in this past reporting context. |
| 177 | B | Data integrity concerns accuracy, consistency and reliability across the data lifecycle. |
| 178 | A | The note makes engineering approval a condition of accepting an alternative. |
| 179 | B | The standard construction is “differ in” a feature or respect. |
| 180 | D | The summary preserves both the reported resolution and the one-week monitoring period without overgeneralizing. |
