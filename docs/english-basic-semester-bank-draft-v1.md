# Basic English — Semester Assessment Bank (Draft v1, Questions 1–20)

Статус: независимый авторский черновик для методической проверки. Не является утверждённым вариантом по конкретному изданию учебника Белова; не импортировать в защищённый банк и не показывать студентам до сверки с рабочей программой и учебником.

Уровень и содержание: ориентир A2–B1, повседневное общение, учёба в колледже, базовая грамматика и чтение. Каждый вопрос имеет один предполагаемый правильный ответ.

## Questions

**1.** My sister usually ___ breakfast at seven o’clock.
- A. has
- B. have
- C. is have
- D. having

**2.** We ___ the college library last Tuesday.
- A. visit
- B. visited
- C. have visit
- D. are visiting

**3.** Mark has lived in this town ___ 2022.
- A. for
- B. during
- C. since
- D. from

**4.** Anna is interested ___ learning foreign languages.
- A. on
- B. at
- C. for
- D. in

**5.** Choose the closest meaning of *reliable*.
- A. Someone or something that can be trusted
- B. Someone who is always late
- C. Something that is difficult to understand
- D. Someone who dislikes working in a team

**6.** You ___ use your phone during the examination. It is not allowed.
- A. don't have to
- B. mustn't
- C. should
- D. may

**7.** This exercise is ___ than the one we did yesterday.
- A. easiest
- B. more easy
- C. easier
- D. the easier

**8.** If it ___ tomorrow, we will have the lesson in the classroom.
- A. rain
- B. will rain
- C. raining
- D. rains

**9.** There isn't ___ milk in the fridge.
- A. many
- B. a few
- C. much
- D. several

**10.** Choose the most polite request.
- A. “Could you explain this word, please?”
- B. “Explain this word now.”
- C. “You explain me this word.”
- D. “What you mean this word?”

### Read the text and answer Questions 11–15.

*Last month, a group of first-year students started a small volunteering project at their college. Every Friday afternoon, they help in the library: they put books back on the shelves, prepare displays and show new students where to find information. The project coordinator, Mr Brown, says that the volunteers are becoming more confident when they speak to visitors. The students do not receive money for this work, but they gain experience and learn to cooperate. Next term, the group plans to organise a book exchange so that students can share books they have already read.*

**11.** When do the students help in the library?
- A. Every Monday morning
- B. Every Friday afternoon
- C. Once a month
- D. Every Saturday evening

**12.** What do the volunteers help new students to do?
- A. Choose their future profession
- B. Find a part-time job
- C. Find information in the library
- D. Prepare for a sports competition

**13.** What change has Mr Brown noticed?
- A. The volunteers are more confident when speaking to visitors
- B. The volunteers have stopped visiting the library
- C. The volunteers receive a monthly salary
- D. The volunteers no longer work together

**14.** In the text, the word *gain* is closest in meaning to:
- A. lose
- B. hide
- C. borrow
- D. get or obtain

**15.** What does the group plan to do next term?
- A. Open a new college building
- B. Cancel the volunteering project
- C. Sell all the library books
- D. Organise a book exchange

**16.** Choose the correct email opening when writing to a teacher whose name you know.
- A. “Hey you!”
- B. “Dear Ms Green,”
- C. “What’s up, teacher?”
- D. “Hi, unknown person.”

**17.** We ___ our English teacher at three o’clock tomorrow; the appointment is already arranged.
- A. meet
- B. met
- C. are meeting
- D. have meet

**18.** Choose the correct word: “Please read the instructions ___ before you begin.”
- A. careful
- B. care
- C. careless
- D. carefully

**19.** Choose the correct question.
- A. “Where does your brother work?”
- B. “Where your brother does work?”
- C. “Where do your brother works?”
- D. “Where is your brother work?”

**20.** I took an umbrella ___ the weather forecast said it might rain.
- A. although
- B. because
- C. unless
- D. while

## Teacher key and rationale

| No. | Key | Rationale |
|---:|:---:|---|
| 1 | A | “My sister” is third-person singular; present simple takes “has”. |
| 2 | B | “Last Tuesday” signals the past simple: “visited”. |
| 3 | C | “Since” is used with a starting point in time. |
| 4 | D | The fixed construction is “interested in”. |
| 5 | A | “Reliable” means able to be trusted or depended on. |
| 6 | B | “Mustn’t” expresses prohibition. |
| 7 | C | The comparative form of “easy” is “easier”. |
| 8 | D | First conditional uses present simple in the if-clause. |
| 9 | C | “Milk” is uncountable; “much” is used in a negative sentence. |
| 10 | A | “Could you…please?” is a polite request. |
| 11 | B | The text says “Every Friday afternoon”. |
| 12 | C | The volunteers show new students where to find information. |
| 13 | A | Mr Brown says they are becoming more confident with visitors. |
| 14 | D | “Gain experience” means get or obtain experience. |
| 15 | D | The final sentence says they plan to organise a book exchange. |
| 16 | B | “Dear Ms Green,” is a suitable formal email opening when the recipient is known. |
| 17 | C | Present continuous can describe a fixed future arrangement. |
| 18 | D | An adverb (“carefully”) modifies the verb “read”. |
| 19 | A | Present simple questions with “your brother” use “does” + base verb. |
| 20 | B | “Because” introduces the reason for taking an umbrella. |
