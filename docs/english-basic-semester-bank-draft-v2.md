# Basic English — Semester Assessment Bank (Draft v2, Questions 21–40)

Статус: независимый авторский черновик для методической проверки. Не импортировать в защищённый банк и не показывать студентам до сверки с рабочей программой и конкретным изданием учебника Белова.

Ориентир: A2–B1. Темы: распорядок колледжа, свободное время, путешествия, здоровье, планы и повседневное общение. Один предполагаемый правильный ответ на вопрос.

## Questions

**21.** We have English lessons ___ Mondays and Thursdays.
- A. at
- B. in
- C. on
- D. by

**22.** There are ___ students in the laboratory today than yesterday.
- A. less
- B. fewer
- C. little
- D. fewest

**23.** I ___ my homework before I watched the film.
- A. finish
- B. have finished
- C. was finishing
- D. had finished

**24.** You look tired. You ___ take a short break.
- A. should
- B. mustn't
- C. can't
- D. would

**25.** My friends enjoy ___ volleyball after classes.
- A. play
- B. to playing
- C. playing
- D. played

**26.** The bus was late, ___ we arrived at college after the first lesson started.
- A. because
- B. so
- C. but
- D. unless

**27.** Choose the correct sentence.
- A. She don't like noisy places.
- B. She doesn't likes noisy places.
- C. She not like noisy places.
- D. She doesn't like noisy places.

**28.** I have never ___ to Scotland, but I would like to visit it.
- A. been
- B. went
- C. be
- D. being

**29.** Could you tell me ___ the nearest bus stop is?
- A. what
- B. who
- C. where
- D. which

**30.** The museum was ___ interesting that we stayed there for three hours.
- A. such
- B. so
- C. too
- D. enough

### Read the text and answer Questions 31–35.

*When Daniel moved to a new city to study, he found it difficult to make friends. At first, he went straight home after classes and spent most evenings online. His tutor suggested joining one of the college clubs. Daniel chose the photography club because he enjoyed taking pictures on his phone. The group meets twice a month and visits different parts of the city. During the first trip, Daniel took photos of old buildings and shared them with the other members. They gave him useful advice and invited him to help prepare a small exhibition. Daniel now feels more comfortable at college and has made several new friends.*

**31.** Why did Daniel find the first weeks difficult?
- A. He did not have enough lessons.
- B. He wanted to leave the city immediately.
- C. He had lost his phone.
- D. He did not know many people in the new city.

**32.** What did Daniel's tutor suggest?
- A. Joining a college club
- B. Changing his course
- C. Finding a weekend job
- D. Moving to another city

**33.** How often does the photography club meet?
- A. Every day
- B. Once a week
- C. Twice a month
- D. Once a year

**34.** What did the other club members do for Daniel?
- A. They bought him a new camera.
- B. They gave him advice and invited him to help with an exhibition.
- C. They asked him to stop taking photographs.
- D. They organised his journey home.

**35.** How has Daniel's situation changed?
- A. He spends every evening alone.
- B. He has stopped attending college.
- C. He wants to sell his photographs.
- D. He feels more comfortable and has made friends.

**36.** Choose the correct future form: “Look at those dark clouds! It ___.”
- A. is going to rain
- B. rains yesterday
- C. has rain
- D. raining

**37.** If I had more free time, I ___ a second language.
- A. will learn
- B. learned
- C. would learn
- D. am learn

**38.** The train ___ at 8:15 tomorrow morning.
- A. leave
- B. leaves
- C. leaving
- D. have left

**39.** Choose the correct response to “Would you mind opening the window?”
- A. Yes, I would mind to.
- B. No, I don't opening.
- C. I am not window.
- D. Not at all.

**40.** Choose the best linking word: “I enjoyed the trip, ___ the weather was cold.”
- A. although
- B. because
- C. therefore
- D. unless

## Teacher key and rationale

| No. | Key | Rationale |
|---:|:---:|---|
| 21 | C | Days of the week take the preposition “on”. |
| 22 | B | “Students” is countable; “fewer” is used for a smaller number. |
| 23 | D | The earlier past action takes the past perfect. |
| 24 | A | “Should” gives advice. |
| 25 | C | “Enjoy” is followed by a gerund. |
| 26 | B | “So” introduces the result of the bus being late. |
| 27 | D | Third-person singular negative: “doesn't” + base verb. |
| 28 | A | Present perfect uses the past participle “been”. |
| 29 | C | “Where” asks about a place. |
| 30 | B | The structure is “so + adjective + that”. |
| 31 | D | The text says he found it difficult to make friends in the new city. |
| 32 | A | His tutor suggested joining a college club. |
| 33 | C | The club meets twice a month. |
| 34 | B | They gave advice and invited him to help prepare an exhibition. |
| 35 | D | The final sentence says he feels more comfortable and has made friends. |
| 36 | A | “Going to” is used for a prediction based on present evidence. |
| 37 | C | Second conditional: “If + past simple, would + infinitive”. |
| 38 | B | Present simple is used for a fixed timetable. |
| 39 | D | “Not at all” is a natural polite response meaning the request is no problem. |
| 40 | A | “Although” introduces a contrast. |
