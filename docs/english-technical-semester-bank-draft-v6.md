# English Technical Semester Assessment — Draft v6 (Questions 101–120)

Статус: авторский черновик для предметной и методической экспертизы. Не импортировать в защищённый банк и не показывать студентам до проверки. Материал проверяет чтение профессиональных текстов и английскую коммуникацию, а не обучает выполнению реальных производственных операций.

## Questions

**101.** The production supervisor asked the team to ___ the cause of the delay.
- A. investigate
- B. investigation
- C. investigative
- D. investigated

**102.** What does *lead time* usually mean in a supply or production context?
- A. The time between placing a request/order and receiving the item
- B. The time an employee spends leading a meeting
- C. The time required to write a job title
- D. The period when a machine is switched off for lunch

**103.** Choose the correct sentence.
- A. “Each component has a unique identification number.”
- B. “Each components have a unique identification number.”
- C. “Each component have unique identification number.”
- D. “Each of component are uniquely identify.”

**104.** A report says: “The sample was contaminated during handling.” What happened?
- A. Unwanted material entered the sample during handling
- B. The sample was measured twice
- C. The sample was delivered early
- D. The sample was approved without testing

**105.** Complete the sentence: “The engineer is responsible ___ reviewing the test documentation.”
- A. for
- B. at
- C. on
- D. with

**106.** Which phrase best describes a *preventive maintenance schedule*?
- A. A planned timetable for maintenance intended to reduce the likelihood of failure
- B. A list of faults discovered after an accident
- C. A record of annual staff holidays
- D. A timetable for product advertising

**107.** Read the instruction in a document-control notice: “Superseded copies must be removed from points of use.” What is the notice asking staff to do?
- A. Remove outdated copies from places where the documents are used
- B. Print more copies of the old revision
- C. Edit the original without authorization
- D. Keep every revision at the workstation for routine use

**108.** Choose the correct form: “The results are more consistent ___ the measurement method was standardized.”
- A. because
- B. despite
- C. unless
- D. whereas

**109.** In a technical context, *to calibrate an instrument* means:
- A. To compare and adjust its measurement performance against a reference
- B. To paint its outer casing
- C. To replace its serial number
- D. To increase its operating temperature

**110.** A table lists planned output as 500 units and actual output as 460 units. Which statement is correct?
- A. Actual output was 40 units below plan
- B. Actual output was 40 units above plan
- C. Actual output matched the plan
- D. Actual output was twice the plan

**111.** Choose the most appropriate phrase for a meeting summary.
- A. “The team agreed to review the test evidence before approving the change.”
- B. “Team agree review evidence yesterday before approve.”
- C. “The evidence is agreeing the change.”
- D. “The team was approval the evidence.”

**112.** What is a *tolerance* in engineering documentation?
- A. The permitted variation from a specified value
- B. The total number of parts in a shipment
- C. The time allowed for a lunch break
- D. The name of a drawing format

**113.** Complete the sentence: “If the network connection is lost, the system ___ the event in its local log.”
- A. records
- B. record
- C. recording
- D. recorded

**114.** Which statement distinguishes a *symptom* from a *cause*?
- A. A symptom is an observed sign of a problem; a cause explains why it occurs
- B. A symptom is always the final solution
- C. A cause is simply another word for a measurement unit
- D. A symptom and a cause always mean exactly the same thing

**115.** Read the delivery update: “The shipment contains 18 items. The packing list identifies 20. Two items are not accounted for.” What is the discrepancy?
- A. Two items listed are missing from the shipment
- B. Two extra items were delivered
- C. The shipment contains exactly the listed quantity
- D. The packing list has no item count

**116.** Choose the correct passive sentence.
- A. “The revised procedure will be approved by the quality manager.”
- B. “The revised procedure will approve by the quality manager.”
- C. “The quality manager will approved the revised procedure by.”
- D. “The revised procedure is approve tomorrow by manager.”

**117.** What does *interoperability* describe?
- A. The ability of systems or devices to work together and exchange information
- B. The ability of a document to be printed in colour
- C. The length of a production shift
- D. The weight of a machine component

**118.** Choose the best completion: “Please ensure that the serial number on the unit ___ the number in the service record.”
- A. matches
- B. match
- C. matching
- D. have matched

**119.** A technical summary states: “The trial demonstrated improved response time under the tested conditions. Further testing is required before the result can be generalized.” Which conclusion is justified?
- A. Improvement was observed in the trial, but broader conclusions require more tests
- B. The system is proven to be better in every possible condition
- C. No improvement was observed
- D. Further testing has been cancelled

**120.** Which information is most important when reporting a software defect so that another team can reproduce it?
- A. Steps to reproduce, expected result, actual result and relevant environment/version
- B. The reporter’s opinion of the software logo
- C. A statement that the program is “bad”
- D. The number of people in the reporter’s office

## Teacher key and rationale

| No. | Key | Rationale |
|---:|:---:|---|
| 101 | A | The infinitive “investigate” follows “asked the team to”. |
| 102 | A | Lead time is the elapsed time from an order/request to delivery or availability. |
| 103 | A | “Each” takes a singular count noun and singular verb; “a unique identification number” is correctly formed. |
| 104 | A | Contamination means unwanted substances or material entered the sample. |
| 105 | A | The fixed construction is “responsible for” + gerund. |
| 106 | A | Preventive maintenance is planned in advance to reduce failure likelihood; actual intervals depend on approved maintenance data. |
| 107 | A | Superseded copies are outdated versions that should not remain at points of use. |
| 108 | A | “Because” introduces the reason for improved consistency. |
| 109 | A | Calibration compares instrument performance with a reference and may include adjustment under a defined process. |
| 110 | A | 500 − 460 = 40; actual output is 40 units below the plan. |
| 111 | A | The sentence is grammatical and appropriate for recording a decision in meeting minutes. |
| 112 | A | Tolerance is the allowable variation from a specified dimension or value. |
| 113 | A | “System” is singular, so the present simple verb is “records”. |
| 114 | A | A symptom is observed evidence; a cause is the underlying reason. |
| 115 | A | The packing list states 20 while only 18 are present, leaving two unaccounted for. |
| 116 | A | Future passive is formed with “will be” + past participle. |
| 117 | A | Interoperability concerns compatible operation and information exchange between systems. |
| 118 | A | “Number” is singular and takes “matches”. |
| 119 | A | The text limits the finding to tested conditions and explicitly requires further testing. |
| 120 | A | Reproducibility requires clear steps, expected/actual outcomes and relevant version/environment details. |
