# Basic English — Semester Assessment Bank (Draft v7, Questions 121–140)

Статус: самостоятельный авторский черновик для методической проверки. Не импортировать в защищённый банк до сверки с рабочей программой и точным изданием учебника Белова.

Ориентир: A2–B1. Темы: профессии, планы, навыки, обслуживание клиентов, экологическая ответственность и чтение. Один предполагаемый правильный ответ на вопрос.

## Questions

**121.** My sister is interested ___ learning another foreign language.
- A. on
- B. in
- C. at
- D. for

**122.** The students ___ their project when the electricity went off.
- A. presented
- B. have presented
- C. were presenting
- D. are presenting

**123.** You should check the address before you ___ the parcel.
- A. send
- B. sent
- C. sending
- D. will sent

**124.** This is the most useful advice I ___ this week.
- A. receive
- B. am receiving
- C. received
- D. have received

**125.** The new employee is responsible for ___ customer requests.
- A. answer
- B. answering
- C. answered
- D. to answering

**126.** There were ___ people at the information desk than we had expected.
- A. less
- B. little
- C. fewer
- D. fewest

**127.** Choose the correct sentence.
- A. She has worked here since three years.
- B. She works here for 2023.
- C. She is working here since 2023.
- D. She has worked here since 2023.

**128.** If you do not understand the task, you ___ the teacher for clarification.
- A. should ask
- B. asked
- C. would asking
- D. have ask

**129.** The meeting was moved to a larger room ___ more participants could attend.
- A. despite
- B. so that
- C. unless
- D. whereas

**130.** The phrase *to gain experience* means:
- A. to lose an opportunity
- B. to refuse a task
- C. to acquire practical knowledge or skill

### Read the text and answer Questions 131–135.

*A college career centre organised a short event where local employers described the skills they expected from new workers. They mentioned technical knowledge, but they also stressed punctuality, clear communication and the ability to learn from feedback. Students could ask questions about interviews and workplace routines. One employer explained that applicants did not need to know every procedure on their first day; however, they were expected to ask when instructions were unclear and to follow safety rules. After the event, the college uploaded a summary and a list of internship contacts to its student portal.*

**131.** What was the purpose of the event?
- A. To give students information about employers' expectations
- B. To select students for a sports team
- C. To replace all college lessons
- D. To teach employers how to use the student portal

**132.** Which skills did employers mention besides technical knowledge?
- A. Drawing and singing
- B. Punctuality and clear communication
- C. Driving and cooking
- D. Speed reading and translation only

**133.** What were new workers expected to do if instructions were unclear?
- A. Ignore the instructions
- B. Leave the workplace
- C. Ask for clarification
- D. Change the safety rules

**134.** What did the college upload after the event?
- A. A list of examination answers
- B. A new timetable for employers
- C. Private interview recordings
- D. A summary and internship contacts

**135.** Which statement best reflects the employer's advice?
- A. New workers must know every procedure immediately.
- B. Asking questions and following safety rules are important.
- C. Technical knowledge is never necessary.
- D. Feedback should always be ignored.

**136.** By the time the course ends, we ___ three practical projects.
- A. complete

- C. will have completed
- D. are complete

**137.** The instructions were too complicated for the visitors ___ without help.
- A. understand
- B. understanding

**138.** Choose the most appropriate response to “Could I speak to the person in charge, please?”
- A. Certainly. I'll see if they are available.
- B. You speak person now.
- C. No, I am speaking yesterday.
- D. It is not my speaking.

**139.** In the text, the word *applicants* refers to people who:
- A. organise college events
- B. apply for a job or position

**140.** We should use reusable bags ___ reduce unnecessary plastic waste.
- A. although
- B. because of

- D. unless

## Teacher key and rationale

| No. | Key | Rationale |
|---:|:---:|---|
| 121 | B | The fixed expression is “interested in”. |
| 122 | C | Past continuous describes an action in progress when another past event occurred. |
| 123 | A | After “before” in a future time clause, present simple is used. |
| 124 | D | “This week” is an unfinished time period; present perfect is appropriate. |
| 125 | B | “Responsible for” is followed by a noun or gerund. |
| 126 | C | “People” is countable plural, so “fewer” is used. |
| 127 | D | Present perfect with “since” introduces a starting point. |
| 128 | A | “Should” expresses advice and takes the bare infinitive. |
| 129 | B | “So that” introduces the purpose or intended result. |
| 130 | C | “Gain experience” means acquire practical knowledge or skill. |
| 131 | A | Employers described the skills and behaviours they expected from new workers. |
| 132 | B | The text explicitly mentions punctuality and clear communication. |
| 133 | C | The employer expected workers to ask when instructions were unclear. |
| 134 | D | The college uploaded a summary and internship contacts. |
| 135 | B | The employer stressed asking questions and following safety rules. |
| 136 | C | Future perfect describes the number of projects completed by a future point. |
| 137 | D | “Too + adjective + for someone + to-infinitive” expresses difficulty. |
| 138 | A | This is a polite and natural response to a request to speak to someone. |
| 139 | B | An applicant is a person who applies for a job or position. |
| 140 | C | “In order to” introduces the purpose of using reusable bags. |
