### Read the text and answer Questions 171–175.

*Many colleges now offer short online courses alongside classroom lessons. Students can watch recorded explanations, complete quizzes and submit assignments through a learning platform. This flexibility is useful for learners who need to revise material at a convenient time. However, online study also requires planning. Teachers recommend setting a regular study schedule, taking short breaks and contacting the tutor when instructions are unclear. A stable internet connection is helpful, but students should also download essential materials when the platform allows it, so they can continue working if their connection fails.*

## Teacher key and rationale

| No. | Key | Rationale |
|---:|:---:|---|
| 161 | A | “Should” gives advice; the verb after it is in the base form. |
| 162 | B | “Since” is used with a starting point in time. |
| 163 | C | Past perfect continuous describes an activity continuing up to a past reference point; the reported question refers to the period before the doctor asked. |
| 164 | D | A modal verb (“must”) is followed by the base form “wear”. |
| 165 | B | “Sugar” is uncountable; “much” is used in a negative sentence. |
| 166 | A | The fixed expression is “good at” + noun or gerund. |
| 167 | C | Past simple passive is used for an action completed in the past. |
| 168 | D | The imperative form gives a direct instruction. |
| 169 | A | “Because” introduces the reason for preferring the library. |
| 170 | B | “Keep fit” means maintain good physical health. |

| 176 | C | Future perfect describes completion before a specified future time. |
