# Technical English — semester assessment bank (authoring draft v1)

Status: **draft for subject review; not connected to the portal and not imported to Google Sheets**.

This first batch contains 20 independently authored items for the provisional Technical English Course 3 / Semester 1 block. It is deliberately separate from the legacy Basic bank. The block checks technical reading, materials, manufacturing workflow, drawings, measurement and professional communication. The course/semester allocation remains provisional until it is checked against the approved work programme.

## Student-facing questions

Each item has four options. Exactly one option is intended to be correct.

### 1. Materials
**Which property describes a material's ability to return to its original shape after the load is removed?**

A. Brittleness  
B. Elasticity  
C. Density  
D. Conductivity

### 2. Materials
**Why is aluminium often selected for aircraft structures?**

A. It is always harder than steel.  
B. It has a favourable strength-to-weight ratio and good corrosion resistance.  
C. It cannot be deformed.  
D. It is a magnetic material.

### 3. Materials
**A metal that can be drawn into a wire is described as…**

A. ductile  
B. brittle  
C. porous  
D. transparent

### 4. Manufacturing
**What is the main purpose of a process sheet in manufacturing?**

A. To advertise the finished product  
B. To list the planned operations and relevant production information  
C. To replace all engineering drawings  
D. To record employees' holiday dates

### 5. Manufacturing
**Which operation normally produces a cylindrical surface by rotating the workpiece against a cutting tool?**

A. Milling  
B. Turning  
C. Broaching  
D. Sheet-metal bending

### 6. Manufacturing
**A milling cutter has several cutting edges and usually…**

A. remains stationary while the workpiece melts  
B. rotates while material is removed from the workpiece  
C. measures the workpiece temperature  
D. joins two parts by adhesive

### 7. Technical drawings
**What does the symbol Ø indicate on a technical drawing?**

A. Radius  
B. Diameter  
C. Surface temperature  
D. Material hardness

### 8. Technical drawings
**A dimension is marked 25.0 ± 0.1 mm. Which interval is acceptable?**

A. 24.9–25.1 mm  
B. 24.0–26.0 mm  
C. 25.1–25.2 mm  
D. 24.8–25.0 mm only

### 9. Measurement
**Which instrument is generally suitable for measuring an external diameter with high precision?**

A. Steel rule  
B. Outside micrometer  
C. Spirit level  
D. Protractor

### 10. Measurement
**The term “resolution” of a measuring instrument refers to…**

A. the largest object it can measure  
B. the smallest change it can indicate  
C. the colour of its display  
D. the time needed to calibrate it

### 11. Quality
**Why is a measuring instrument calibrated?**

A. To make it look new  
B. To establish its measurement performance in relation to known standards  
C. To increase the size of every measured part  
D. To remove the need for inspection

### 12. Quality
**A measured value is outside the drawing tolerance. What is the most appropriate first action?**

A. Change the drawing without approval  
B. Hide the result  
C. Identify and report the non-conformity according to the workplace process  
D. Send the part to the customer immediately

### 13. Aviation context
**What is the primary function of an aircraft fuselage?**

A. To house the crew, passengers or payload and connect major aircraft sections  
B. To generate all electrical power  
C. To control engine fuel quality  
D. To measure runway length

### 14. Aviation context
**In an aircraft, the wing primarily generates…**

A. lift  
B. cabin pressure  
C. electrical resistance  
D. braking force

### 15. Technical vocabulary
**In a specification, “shall” most commonly expresses…**

A. a mandatory requirement  
B. a personal preference  
C. a past event  
D. an uncertain possibility

### 16. Technical vocabulary
**Choose the best meaning of “inspect the component for cracks”.**

A. Paint the component  
B. Examine the component to find cracks  
C. Replace the component with a larger one  
D. Store the component in a box

### 17. Workplace communication
**A colleague gives an unclear measurement over the phone. What is the best response?**

A. “I will guess the value.”  
B. “Could you repeat the value and confirm the unit, please?”  
C. “The unit does not matter.”  
D. “I will ignore the message.”

### 18. Workplace communication
**Which sentence is the clearest neutral report of a technical problem?**

A. “The machine is terrible.”  
B. “Someone has done everything wrong.”  
C. “The measured diameter is 0.3 mm above the upper limit.”  
D. “This part looks strange.”

### 19. Reading a specification
**A drawing revision changes from Rev. B to Rev. C. What should a technician check before using the drawing?**

A. Whether Rev. C is the current approved revision  
B. Whether Rev. B has a nicer title block  
C. Whether the paper is the same colour  
D. Whether the drawing was printed on a Friday

### 20. Safe professional practice
**Why should a worker stop and ask for clarification when a technical instruction is incomplete?**

A. To avoid making an unsupported assumption that could affect quality or safety  
B. To finish the task without reading the instruction  
C. To change the specification independently  
D. To avoid documenting the work

## Teacher key and rationale (do not expose to students)

| Item | Key | Rationale |
|---:|:---:|---|
| 1 | B | Elasticity is the ability to recover shape after unloading, within the elastic range. |
| 2 | B | Aircraft material selection considers mass, strength and corrosion behaviour; the other options are absolute or incorrect. |
| 3 | A | Ductility is the ability to undergo tensile deformation, including drawing into wire. |
| 4 | B | A process sheet communicates the planned sequence and production details. |
| 5 | B | In turning, the workpiece rotates and a cutting tool removes material. |
| 6 | B | Milling uses a rotating multi-edge cutter. |
| 7 | B | Ø is the conventional diameter symbol. |
| 8 | A | The tolerance gives 25.0 − 0.1 to 25.0 + 0.1 mm. |
| 9 | B | An outside micrometer is designed for precise external measurements. |
| 10 | B | Resolution is the smallest increment/change an instrument can display or distinguish. |
| 11 | B | Calibration relates instrument indications to reference standards and known values. |
| 12 | C | A non-conformity must be contained and reported through the authorised process. |
| 13 | A | The fuselage is the main body that accommodates crew/passengers or payload and joins major sections. |
| 14 | A | The wing generates lift in normal fixed-wing flight. |
| 15 | A | In technical specifications, “shall” normally marks a requirement. |
| 16 | B | “Inspect … for cracks” means examine the component to detect cracks. |
| 17 | B | Repeating the value and unit is a read-back/confirmation technique. |
| 18 | C | It reports an observable measurement against a limit without blame or vague judgement. |
| 19 | A | Work must use the current approved revision to avoid obsolete information. |
| 20 | A | Clarification prevents unsafe or quality-critical assumptions. |
