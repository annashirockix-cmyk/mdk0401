# English Technical Semester Assessment — Draft v7 (Questions 121–140)

Статус: черновик для предметной и методической экспертизы. Не импортировать в защищённый банк и не показывать студентам до проверки. Задания проверяют английский язык в инженерном контексте, а не дают инструкции по эксплуатации оборудования.

## Questions

**121.** Choose the correct completion: “The inspection report ___ the condition of the component at the time of examination.”
- A. describes
- B. describe
- C. describing
- D. have describe

**122.** What does *out of specification* mean?
- A. A result or characteristic is outside the permitted requirements
- B. A product has been shipped to the wrong building
- C. A document has no title
- D. A machine is operating without an operator

**123.** Complete the sentence: “The supplier notified us ___ a change in the delivery date.”
- A. between
- B. during
- C. across
- D. about

**124.** A drawing note says “Do not scale the drawing.” What does this mean?
- A. Do not determine dimensions by measuring the drawing image; use stated dimensions
- B. Enlarge the drawing until all dimensions are visible
- C. Convert every dimension into a different unit
- D. Remove the scale information from the title block

**125.** Choose the most appropriate phrase for a formal request.
- A. “Give latest paper now.”
- B. “Could you provide the latest revision of the interface specification?”
- C. “You must sending me interface.”
- D. “I want that you provides newest.”

**126.** What is a *bill of materials (BOM)*?
- A. A list of employee salaries
- B. A structured list of materials, parts or components required for a product or assembly
- C. A record of customer complaints only
- D. A schedule of public holidays

**127.** Complete the sentence: “Neither the drawing nor the accompanying notes ___ the revised connector type.”
- A. specifies
- B. specify
- C. specifying
- D. has specify

**128.** A maintenance log states: “The unit was returned to service after the inspection was completed and the record was signed.” What can be concluded?
- A. The unit was returned before any inspection
- B. The record was left unsigned
- C. The inspection was cancelled
- D. The log records a return to service after inspection and sign-off

**129.** Which word is closest in meaning to *feasible* in “a feasible design proposal”?
- A. Decorative
- B. Unrelated
- C. Temporary
- D. Practical

**130.** Choose the correct completion: “The test was postponed ___ the required reference sample had not arrived.”
- A. although
- B. unless
- C. because
- D. so that

**131.** What does *ambient temperature* refer to?
- A. The temperature of the surrounding environment
- B. The temperature inside every electronic component
- C. The maximum temperature specified for a material
- D. The temperature used only during welding

**132.** Read the status update: “Revision C is current. Revision B is retained for historical reference only.” Which statement is correct?
- A. Revision B replaces Revision C
- B. Both revisions are current operating instructions
- C. Revision C is the current version; Revision B is kept as a record
- D. Revision C has been withdrawn

**133.** Complete the sentence: “The data ___ collected from three separate test runs.”
- A. was
- B. were
- C. is
- D. has

**134.** In an aviation maintenance context, what is the purpose of a *technical log entry*?
- A. To advertise the airline's services
- B. To replace the aircraft's approved maintenance programme
- C. To record passenger meal preferences
- D. To record relevant technical status, defects or maintenance information in the required record

**135.** Which sentence clearly reports an observed fact rather than an unsupported assumption?
- A. “The circuit is probably unhappy.”
- B. “The component must be poor because it looks old.”
- C. “The measured voltage was 4.8 V at the test point.”
- D. “The system always fails for no reason.”

**136.** Choose the correct completion: “Please compare the measured values ___ the limits stated in the specification.”
- A. from
- B. with
- C. at
- D. by

**137.** What does *root-cause analysis* aim to identify?
- A. The person who wrote the report, regardless of the issue
- B. The colour of the equipment
- C. The underlying reason or reasons for a problem
- D. The number of pages in a manual

**138.** A CAD drawing uses separate layers for dimensions, centre lines and annotations. What is the main benefit?
- A. Different drawing information can be organized and managed separately
- B. The drawing no longer needs dimensions
- C. All geometry is automatically converted into a 3D model
- D. The file cannot be edited

**139.** Complete the sentence: “By the time the review meeting started, the team ___ the test results.”
- A. has check
- B. will checking
- C. had checked
- D. have checking

**140.** A report says: “The sensor output remained stable during the 30-minute observation period.” Which statement is supported?
- A. The sensor will remain stable under all future conditions
- B. The sensor was not connected
- C. The observation lasted 30 hours
- D. No change in sensor output was observed during that period

## Teacher key and rationale

| No. | Key | Rationale |
|---:|:---:|---|
| 121 | A | Singular subject “report” takes “describes”. |
| 122 | A | “Out of specification” means outside stated acceptance limits or requirements. |
| 123 | D | The natural collocation is “notify someone about” a change. |
| 124 | A | Dimensions should be taken from stated values, not estimated from the printed or displayed image. |
| 125 | B | This is a polite, grammatically correct formal request. |
| 126 | B | A BOM lists the materials and components needed for a product or assembly. |
| 127 | B | With the nearer plural subject “notes”, the verb is plural: “specify”. Check the chosen grammar reference's notional/agreement guidance for neither…nor. |
| 128 | D | The statement gives the sequence: inspection completed, record signed, unit returned to service. |
| 129 | D | “Feasible” means capable of being done or put into practice; “practical” is the closest option. |
| 130 | C | “Because” introduces the reason for postponement. |
| 131 | A | Ambient temperature is the temperature of the surrounding environment. |
| 132 | C | The notice explicitly identifies C as current and B as historical. |
| 133 | B | In this technical context, “data” is treated as a plural noun; “were collected” is the expected form. Check local style guide if it treats “data” as a mass singular. |
| 134 | D | A technical log records relevant aircraft technical status and maintenance information under applicable procedures. |
| 135 | C | It reports a measurable value and location without speculation. |
| 136 | B | “Compare … with” is the standard construction here. |
| 137 | C | Root-cause analysis seeks underlying causes, not merely visible symptoms. |
| 138 | A | Layers organize drawing elements so categories can be displayed, edited or managed separately. |
| 139 | C | Past perfect describes the earlier completed action before another past event. |
| 140 | D | The evidence is limited to the stated 30-minute observation period. |
