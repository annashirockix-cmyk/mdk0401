# English Technical Semester Assessment — Draft v5 (Questions 81–100)

Статус: авторский черновик для предметной и методической экспертизы. Не импортировать в защищённый банк и не показывать студентам до проверки. Задания проверяют английский язык профессионального общения и чтения; они не являются эксплуатационными инструкциями.

## Questions

**81.** The operator must report any unusual vibration ___ it causes further damage.
- A. before
- B. although
- C. unless
- D. despite

**82.** Choose the best definition of *downtime* in manufacturing.
- A. The period when equipment is unavailable for production
- B. The time required to train a new employee
- C. The time when a machine operates at full capacity
- D. The interval between two staff meetings

**83.** Complete the sentence: “The software update ___ the error message that appeared during startup.”
- A. has resolved
- B. have resolve
- C. resolving
- D. resolve

**84.** A technical drawing includes the note “Do not scale”. What does it mean?
- A. The drawing must be printed at twice its size
- B. All dimensions are approximate
- C. Do not take dimensions by measuring the drawing; use stated dimensions
- D. The component has no specified dimensions

**85.** Choose the correct word: “The inspection team found no visible ___ on the surface.”
- A. defective
- B. defectively
- C. defecting
- D. defect

**86.** Read the message: “The replacement part has not arrived. The repair is therefore postponed until the delivery is confirmed.” Why is the repair postponed?
- A. The repair has been completed
- B. The replacement part has not arrived
- C. The delivery has been cancelled permanently
- D. The inspection team is unavailable

**87.** Which phrase is the most appropriate way to ask a colleague to confirm a technical detail?
- A. “You know connector, yes?”
- B. “Tell me that thing immediately.”
- C. “Could you confirm the connector type shown in the latest drawing?”
- D. “I am confirm connector yesterday.”

**88.** Complete the sentence: “The results ___ in the maintenance database after the inspection.”
- A. were entered
- B. entered
- C. was entering
- D. are enter

**89.** In a process description, *subsequently* means:
- A. At the same time
- B. Afterwards
- C. For this reason
- D. In contrast

**90.** A graph shows that energy consumption increased from 120 kWh to 150 kWh. Which statement is accurate?
- A. Consumption fell by 30 kWh
- B. Consumption rose by 30 kWh
- C. Consumption remained unchanged
- D. Consumption doubled

**91.** Choose the correct sentence.
- A. “The equipment have tested twice since repair.”
- B. “The equipment has been tested twice since the repair.”
- C. “The equipment was test twice since the repair.”
- D. “The equipment has testing twice after repair.”

**92.** What is the main purpose of a *root-cause analysis*?
- A. To assign a colour to each component
- B. To estimate an employee’s annual leave
- C. To replace all routine inspections
- D. To identify the underlying reason for a problem

**93.** A programmer writes: “IF temperature > limit THEN alarm = ON”. What does the condition mean?
- A. The alarm is always off
- B. The temperature is reduced by the program
- C. The limit is equal to the alarm state
- D. The alarm is activated when the temperature is greater than the limit

**94.** Choose the correct form: “The new components are ___ compatible with the existing assembly.”
- A. full
- B. fullness
- C. fulfilling
- D. fully

**95.** Read the handover note: “Job 218 is complete. The test results are attached. One item remains open: the updated diagram must be added to the record.” What remains to be done?
- A. Repeat the entire job
- B. Remove the test results
- C. Close the job without the diagram
- D. Add the updated diagram to the record

**96.** Which term describes the ability of a system to continue operating when one component fails?
- A. Surface finish
- B. Fault tolerance
- C. Batch number
- D. Thermal expansion

**97.** Complete the sentence: “By the time the engineer arrived, the test ___.”
- A. has finish
- B. is finishing
- C. had finished
- D. will finished

**98.** What does *obsolete documentation* mean?
- A. Documentation that is out of date and no longer current
- B. A document that has been approved today
- C. A document containing only photographs
- D. A document that is still under translation

**99.** Choose the most precise report statement.
- A. “The pressure was bad many times.”
- B. “Something happened with pressure.”
- C. “The pressure reading exceeded the stated limit in three of five recorded checks.”
- D. “The limit exceeded the pressure by the machine.”

**100.** A team compares two software versions before approving a release. Which information is most useful in the change record?
- A. The names of unrelated visitors
- B. A general statement that the software looks better
- C. Version identifiers, change summary, test evidence and approval status
- D. The colour of the computer case

## Teacher key and rationale

| No. | Key | Rationale |
|---:|:---:|---|
| 81 | A | “Before” expresses the required reporting point in relation to further damage. |
| 82 | A | Downtime is a period when equipment is unavailable for its intended production function. |
| 83 | A | “Has resolved” is present perfect, appropriate for a completed update with a current result. |
| 84 | C | The note directs the reader to use written dimensions rather than measuring the drawing. |
| 85 | D | A noun is required after “visible”; “defect” is the correct form. |
| 86 | B | The message directly identifies the missing replacement part as the reason for postponement. |
| 87 | C | The wording is polite, specific and identifies the technical detail to be confirmed. |
| 88 | A | The passive past form is appropriate because results receive the action. |
| 89 | B | “Subsequently” means afterwards or at a later stage. |
| 90 | B | 150 − 120 = 30 kWh; the statement describes the increase accurately. |
| 91 | B | “Equipment” is uncountable and takes “has”; the present perfect passive is correctly formed. |
| 92 | D | Root-cause analysis seeks the underlying cause rather than only describing a symptom. |
| 93 | D | The condition is true when the temperature is greater than the defined limit, so the alarm state is set to ON. |
| 94 | D | The adverb “fully” modifies the adjective “compatible”. |
| 95 | D | The note explicitly identifies adding the updated diagram as the remaining action. |
| 96 | B | Fault tolerance is the ability to continue functioning despite a component fault. |
| 97 | C | Past perfect marks the test completion before another past event. |
| 98 | A | Obsolete documentation is superseded or out of date and should not be mistaken for a current controlled version. |
| 99 | C | The statement is measurable, specific and tied to recorded evidence. |
| 100 | C | A useful change record identifies versions, changes, verification evidence and approval state. |
