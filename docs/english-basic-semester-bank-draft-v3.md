# Basic English — Semester Assessment Bank (Draft v3, Questions 41–60)

Статус: самостоятельный авторский черновик для методической проверки. Не импортировать в защищённый банк и не показывать студентам до сверки с рабочей программой и точным изданием учебника Белова.

Ориентир: A2–B1. Темы: повседневные обязанности, здоровье, поездки, обучение, общение и чтение. Каждый вопрос рассчитан на один правильный ответ.

### Read the text and answer Questions 51–55.

*Last spring, a college group organised a two-day trip to a nearby nature reserve. Before leaving, the students checked the weather forecast, prepared reusable water bottles and read the visitor rules. A guide explained that visitors should stay on marked paths because rare plants grow in the surrounding area. During the trip, the group collected information for a college project but did not remove plants or disturb animals. On the second day, heavy rain began earlier than expected. The guide changed the route and led everyone to a sheltered visitor centre. After returning to college, the students used their notes and photographs to prepare a short presentation about responsible tourism.*

**51.** How long was the college trip?
- A. One afternoon
- B. Two days
- C. One week
- D. Three weeks

**52.** Why did the guide ask visitors to stay on marked paths?
- A. The paths were shorter.
- B. The group had to reach a railway station.
- C. To avoid damaging rare plants in the area.

**53.** What did the students collect during the trip?

**54.** Why was the route changed on the second day?

**55.** What did the students prepare after the trip?

## Teacher key and rationale

| No. | Key | Rationale |
|---:|:---:|---|
| 41 | A | “Usually” describes a routine, so the present simple is used. |
| 42 | A | “Enough” means the number is sufficient; “chairs” is countable plural. |
| 43 | B | “Because” introduces the reason for the cancellation. |
| 44 | C | “Should” is followed by the base form of the verb. |
| 45 | A | “Miss a meeting” means arrive too late to attend it. |
| 46 | C | “Since” introduces the starting point of a period continuing to the present. |
| 47 | D | Backshift changes “am” to “was” and “I” to “she”. |
| 48 | A | First conditional uses present simple in the if-clause. |
| 49 | B | The comparative form of “modern” is “more modern”. |
| 50 | C | “Look forward to” is followed by a noun or gerund. |
| 51 | B | The first sentence says the trip lasted two days. |
| 52 | C | The text says rare plants grow in the surrounding area; staying on marked paths helps protect them. |
| 53 | A | The group collected information for a college project. |
| 54 | D | Heavy rain began earlier than expected. |
| 55 | B | The final sentence says they prepared a presentation about responsible tourism. |
| 56 | C | Present perfect questions use “Have” with “you”. |
| 57 | B | Modal passive structure: “must + be + past participle”. |
| 58 | A | This is a natural polite response to an apology. |
| 59 | B | A sheltered place offers protection from bad weather. |
| 60 | C | “Otherwise” introduces a possible negative result if they do not leave. |
