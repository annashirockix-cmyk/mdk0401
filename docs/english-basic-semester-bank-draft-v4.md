## Questions

**61.** I have not finished the assignment ___; I need another ten minutes.
- A. already
- B. yet
- C. ever
- D. just

**62.** The students were asked ___ their phones before the test began.
- A. switch off
- B. switching off
- C. to switch off
- D. switched off

**63.** This is the library ___ I borrowed the book about astronomy.
- A. where
- B. which
- C. whose
- D. who

**64.** Tom and his friends ___ available after classes today.
- A. is
- B. was
- C. has
- D. are

**65.** The instructions were written clearly, so everyone could understand them ___.
- A. easy
- B. easily
- C. easier
- D. ease

**66.** We had to wait for the bus ___ nearly forty minutes.
- A. since
- B. by
- C. for
- D. during

- A. English is spoken in many countries.
- B. English speaks in many countries.

- A. отказаться от

### Read the text and answer Questions 71–75.

*When the college introduced a digital timetable, students could check lesson changes on their phones or on screens near the main entrance. At first, some students continued to rely on printed timetables because they were not used to checking updates online. The college then organised short demonstrations during class meetings. Students learned how to receive notifications and report incorrect information. After a month, fewer students arrived at the wrong room, and teachers said that last-minute changes were easier to communicate. The college still keeps a printed noticeboard for visitors who do not use the digital system.*

**71.** What did the college introduce?
- A. A new examination system
- B. A digital timetable
- C. A printed textbook
- D. A system for paying fees

**72.** Why did some students keep using printed timetables at first?
- A. They did not have any lessons.
- B. The digital timetable showed no information.
- C. They were not accustomed to checking online updates.
- D. Teachers asked them to avoid their phones.

**73.** What did the demonstrations teach students to do?
- A. Receive notifications and report incorrect information
- B. Change their examination marks
- C. Create new college courses
- D. Repair the screens near the entrance

**74.** What happened after a month?
- A. The college removed every printed notice.
- B. More students arrived at the wrong room.
- C. Teachers stopped changing lesson times.
- D. Fewer students went to the wrong room.

**75.** Why does the college keep a printed noticeboard?
- A. To advertise mobile phones
- B. For visitors who do not use the digital system
- C. To replace the digital timetable for students
- D. To publish examination answers

**76.** I wish I ___ more time to practise speaking English.
- A. have

**77.** By the time we arrived at the station, the train ___.
- A. had already left

**78.** Choose the most appropriate phrase to ask someone to clarify information.

**79.** The word *rely on* is closest in meaning to:

## Teacher key and rationale

| No. | Key | Rationale |
|---:|:---:|---|
| 61 | B | “Yet” is commonly used in negative present perfect sentences. |
| 62 | C | “Ask someone to do something” takes the infinitive with “to”. |
| 63 | A | “Where” introduces a relative clause referring to a place. |
| 64 | D | The compound subject “Tom and his friends” takes a plural verb. |
| 65 | B | The adverb “easily” describes how the instructions could be understood. |
| 66 | C | “For” introduces a duration of time. |
| 67 | A | Present simple passive: “is + past participle”. |
| 68 | C | Second conditional: “If + past simple, would + infinitive”. |
| 69 | B | “So” introduces the result of the museum being closed. |
| 70 | D | “Take part in” means participate in an activity. |
| 71 | B | The first sentence states that the college introduced a digital timetable. |
| 72 | C | The text says they were not used to checking updates online. |
| 73 | A | The demonstrations covered notifications and reporting incorrect information. |
| 74 | D | The text says fewer students arrived at the wrong room. |
| 75 | B | The final sentence gives this reason explicitly. |
| 76 | C | “Wish” about an unreal present situation is followed by a past form. |
| 77 | A | The train left before the later past event; past perfect is appropriate. |
| 78 | D | This is a polite and clear request for clarification. |
| 79 | B | “Rely on” means depend on or trust. |
| 80 | A | “Because” introduces the reason for postponing the meeting. |
